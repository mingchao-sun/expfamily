# Notices

The methods are written from the published formulas (Lin, 2013; Fisher, 1935; Westfall and Young, 1993; Romano and Wolf, 2005; Eldridge et al., 2006) and checked against the R packages `estimatr` (MIT), `ri2` (MIT), `randomizr` (MIT) and `pwr` (GPL-3), which are not included.
The module uses only base R and `ggplot2`.
The sample data are simulated.
